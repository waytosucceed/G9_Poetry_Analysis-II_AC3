Proverbs on “Blood”

“You cannot get blood from a stone”: It is impossible to obtain something (especially money, help, or resources) from someone or something that does not have it to give.
Example: The creditors kept demanding payment, but he reminded them that you cannot get blood from a stone.

“Blood will out”: A person’s true nature, heritage, or family traits will eventually reveal themselves, regardless of attempts to hide them.
Example: Despite his modest upbringing, his aristocratic manner proved that blood will out.
